Your music folders, played as they are.

NuVa is a small, free music player for the Mac. It plays FLAC through Apple's own audio engine, sends it to your AirPlay speakers, and browses your folders and your Jellyfin server exactly as you named them.

NuVa for Android TV is the same small, free player for the living room. It plays your Jellyfin server by its real folders, FLAC and 24-bit included, through Android's own player.

NuVa for Windows is the same small, free player for the PC. It plays FLAC through Windows' own decoders, and browses your folders, network shares and Jellyfin server exactly as you named them.

why

Made for music kept in folders

You spent years naming your folders and files just right. Most players throw that away and rebuild your library from tags, so remixes, covers and live sets end up scattered across albums you've never heard of. NuVa doesn't.

Your folders, untouched

A folder is an album, a file is a track. No import, no tag scanning, no library rebuilt behind your back. Natural sort order, so track 2 comes before track 10.

Your server's folders, untouched

NuVa browses Jellyfin by the folders on the server's disk, never by its tags. A folder is an album, a file is a track, in natural order: track 2 comes before track 10.

FLAC on a Mac, natively

Apple's Music app won't open FLAC. NuVa plays FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3 and AAC through AVFoundation, the engine behind QuickTime. No bundled codecs, no surprises over Bluetooth.

FLAC on the TV, natively

FLAC, 24-bit included, MP3 and AAC, through Android's own player and decoders. NuVa streams the original file, so a FLAC stays a FLAC.

AirPlay 2 to your hi-fi

Send the sound to a WiiM streamer, a HomePod, an Apple TV or any AirPlay 2 speaker: click where the display says the sound is going.

The remote does everything

Play, pause, skip and seek from the remote. Hold OK to add a folder or a track to the queue, or to move and remove it; Back asks before leaving.

Jellyfin, by folder

Sign in to your Jellyfin server and browse it by its real folders, not its tags. NuVa streams the original file, so a FLAC stays a FLAC.

An equalizer and a spectrum

Ten bands and eight presets, opened from EQ on the display. The spectrum on the display moves with what you hear.

Reopens where you were

Turn the TV back on and NuVa is on the same folder, with the same queue and the same track.

Free, no ads

Free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads and no analytics. Your music stays on your server.

A NAS, a disk or a CD

Folders on your Mac, an external disk or a NAS over SMB. Put in an audio CD and NuVa names the album and its tracks from MusicBrainz.

Keyboard first

Media keys, Control Center, AirPods and a shortcut for everything. Close the window and the music goes on in a mini player in the menu bar.

FLAC on Windows, natively

FLAC, 24-bit included, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3 and AAC through Media Foundation, Windows' own decoders. No bundled codecs, no VLC, no ffmpeg.

Any output device

Choose the speakers, headphones or DAC where the display says the sound is going; Windows has no AirPlay.

A NAS, a disk or a CD

Folders on your PC, an external disk or a network share. Put in an audio CD and NuVa names the album and its tracks from MusicBrainz.

Keyboard first

Media keys, the Windows media controls, taskbar buttons and a Ctrl shortcut for everything. Close the window and the music goes on in a mini player in the tray.

questions

Questions and answers

How do I play FLAC files on a Mac?

Apple's Music app doesn't open FLAC. Install NuVa, add the folder that holds your music and double-click an album. NuVa plays FLAC, hi-res 24-bit files included, through Apple's own audio engine, with no conversion.

Is NuVa a Jellyfin client for Mac?

Yes. Sign in to your Jellyfin server with your own account and NuVa browses it by the server's real folders, not by Jellyfin's tags. It streams the original files, so a FLAC stays a FLAC. The password is kept in the macOS Keychain.

Can NuVa play to a WiiM or another AirPlay speaker?

Yes. NuVa supports AirPlay 2: click the output name on its display and choose a WiiM streamer, a HomePod, an Apple TV or any AirPlay speaker.

Does NuVa read tags or reorganise my library?

No. A folder is an album and a file is a track. NuVa shows folder and file names exactly as they are and sorts tracks naturally by file name, so track 2 comes before track 10.

Can I play music from a NAS?

Yes. Connect to the share in Finder (SMB) and add the folder in NuVa; it comes back by itself when the share is mounted again. You can also use a Jellyfin server on the NAS. Plex and UPnP/DLNA servers aren't supported yet.

Is NuVa free?

Yes. NuVa is free and open source under the MIT license, with no account, no ads and no analytics.

Why does macOS say it can't check NuVa?

NuVa isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate. After dragging it to Applications and opening it once, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to the NuVa message. You only do this once.

Is there NuVa for Android TV?

Yes. NuVa for Android TV runs on an Android TV box or a TV with Android TV 8.1 or later and plays a Jellyfin server by its folders, FLAC included, with the equalizer, the spectrum and the ambient faces. Install it with the Downloader app: type nuva.martinsnuno.com/tv.

Is there NuVa for Windows?

Yes. NuVa for Windows runs on Windows 10 (2004) or 11 and plays folders, network shares, Jellyfin and audio CDs, FLAC through Windows' own decoders, with the equalizer, the spectrum and the ambient faces; it has no AirPlay. Download NuVa-Windows.zip, unzip it and open NuVa.exe; Windows asks once, under More info, to run it anyway.

Which Macs does NuVa run on?

Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, with Apple silicon or Intel.

open source

Built in the open

NuVa's code is on GitHub under the MIT license, in Swift and SwiftUI with no dependencies. Report a bug or suggest an idea in the issues, or send a pull request. To build it you need Xcode 16 and XcodeGen.

brew install xcodegen
xcodegen generate
open NuVa.xcodeproj

NuVa's code is on GitHub under the MIT license. The Android TV app is in Kotlin, in the android folder, with Jetpack Compose and AndroidX Media3. Report a bug or suggest an idea in the issues, or send a pull request. To build it you need JDK 17 and the Android SDK.

cd android
./gradlew assembleDebug

NuVa's code is on GitHub under the MIT license. The Windows app is in C# and WPF, in the windows folder, with no NuGet packages; it builds on a Mac too. Report a bug or suggest an idea in the issues, or send a pull request. To build it you need the .NET 10 SDK.

brew install dotnet
scripts/make-windows-zip.sh
